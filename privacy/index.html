Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026 · Zegree

In short: your photos, videos, files and contacts are encrypted on your iPhone. We never see them and we never hold a key that could open them. The app sends only anonymous usage counts.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you add to Vaultbird (photos, videos, files and contacts) is encrypted on your iPhone before it is saved. Only your passcode or your 24-word paper recovery key can open it. Vaultbird has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. We never see your items, and there is no developer key, master key or copy of your key held by us or anyone else.

Your iCloud backup (optional)

If you turn on backup, Vaultbird stores an end-to-end encrypted copy of your vault in your own iCloud, in Apple's CloudKit private database for your Apple Account. It is encrypted on your iPhone before upload and only your paper recovery key can open it. Item sizes are padded, so the backup does not reveal what kind of items you keep. We cannot see, open or delete your backup; it is part of your iCloud account.

After Delete Vault, the encrypted backup is kept for 30 days so you can still restore it with your paper key, then it is deleted the next time Vaultbird opens.

What is collected, how and why

Only anonymous usage counts. Vaultbird uses one third-party service:

  • TelemetryDeck receives anonymous counts of which screens and features are used (for example "backup turned on" or "restore drill passed"), plus coarse app details it adds itself (app version, iOS version, language). The app sends them over an encrypted connection with a random ID that changes every month. They never include names, your photos, videos, files or contacts, how many items you keep, anything you type, your passcode or recovery key. Your IP address is not stored. The counts are not linked to you and are not used for tracking or advertising. Nothing at all is sent while a decoy vault is open. TelemetryDeck Privacy Policy · TelemetryDeck Terms.

We use these counts only to learn which features help people and where setup or backup goes wrong, so we can improve the app. TelemetryDeck processes them under its own privacy policy and protects them at least as well as this policy does.

Apple crash reports. If you allow sharing with app developers in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and app usage numbers with us. You can turn this off there at any time. They never contain your vault's contents, names or keys.

There are no ads, no advertising ID and no tracking.

Permissions

  • Photos: adding photos and videos uses the system picker and needs no permission. Vaultbird asks for access only to remove originals from Photos after they are safely in your vault, and for add-only access only when you move items back to Photos.
  • Camera: used only when you take photos or videos straight into your vault, and for the optional break-in photo after wrong passcodes if you turn it on. What it takes goes into your encrypted vault, never into Photos, and is never sent anywhere.
  • Microphone: used only while you film a video straight into your vault, for that video's sound.
  • Contacts: adding contacts uses the system picker and needs no permission. Vaultbird asks for access only when you move a contact back to the Contacts app.

Keeping and deleting data

  • On your iPhone: your vault is removed when you delete the app, or at once with Settings › Delete Vault.
  • iCloud backup: kept 30 days after Delete Vault, then deleted. You can also remove it in iPhone Settings › your name › iCloud.
  • Usage counts: kept according to TelemetryDeck's retention. Because they are anonymous, they cannot be traced back to you.
  • To stop sending: delete the app. Nothing is sent after that.
  • Requests and questions: email support@zegree.com.

Children

Vaultbird does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. The anonymous usage counts above are the same for every person.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new effective date before the app version that needs it is released.

Contact

Vaultbird is made by Zegree. Questions about privacy: support@zegree.com.
