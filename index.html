Peace of mind

The everyday worries, handled

  • Know your backup works

    A seal on the home screen shows your backup is in place. Once a month every backed-up item is checked, and anything damaged is repaired.

  • Nothing leaks to your phone

    Nothing from the vault appears in Spotlight, Siri suggestions, Files recents, Share suggestions, widgets, Contacts or caller ID, unless you move it out.

  • Guards against glances

    Blurred thumbnails if you like, a cover in the app switcher and while the screen is recorded, lock when you put it face down or shake it, and auto-lock.

  • A decoy for when you're pressed

    A second passcode opens a separate, harmless vault. Nothing on your iPhone records that it was set up.

  • Know who tried

    Turn on the break-in photo, and after 3 wrong passcodes the front camera takes one photo, kept inside your vault.

  • Undo for mistakes

    Deleted items wait 30 days in Recently Deleted. After Delete Vault, your iCloud copy stays 30 days and your paper key still opens it.

How it stays yours

From your iPhone to a new one, and only with your key

  1. 1

    Locked on this iPhone

    Everything is encrypted before it is saved. Only your passcode or your paper key opens it.

  2. 2

    Copied to your own iCloud, end to end encrypted

    Encrypted on your iPhone before upload, in your Apple Account's private iCloud. We can't see or open it.

  3. 3

    The restore drill proves it comes back

    Type your paper key and Vaultbird checks that every backed-up item opens, without changing anything on your iPhone.

  4. 4

    Your 24-word paper key brings everything back on a new iPhone

    Same iCloud account, Restore from iCloud Backup, type the 24 words. Everything returns.

Questions

Short answers

More answers on Support

I forgot my passcode

On the lock screen tap Forgot Passcode? and type the 24 words from your paper recovery key. Your vault opens and you choose a new passcode. Nothing is lost.

I have a new iPhone

Install Vaultbird on the new iPhone, signed in to the same iCloud account, and choose Restore from iCloud Backup. Type your 24-word paper key and everything comes back. A vault lives on one iPhone at a time.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in, no email and no account with us. Your vault lives on your iPhone, and its backup lives in your own iCloud.
